Hover over the chart for the count Z and σ at a point.
Each point marches λ = iω, ω ∈ [10⁻⁹, ωmax], unwrapping arg D with one evaluation of D and dD/dω (complex dual numbers) per step and step control from the trapezoid phase prediction; Z = n/2 − Δarg D/π counts the roots with Re λ > 0, and the deepest |D| minima give first-order estimates of the roots near the axis (σ = the rightmost). Large charts run as several row bands so no single GPU submission is long. Validation against the Julia engine · Full-HD benchmark
